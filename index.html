How it works

1. Listen

Uses simple text cues in English, Tamil or Hindi. Optional speech recognition is browser-dependent; face preview is not analyzed.

2. Show a demo signal

Simple keyword cues update a 0–100 display. This is not a mental-health assessment.

3. Support

Music, breathing and games are matched to your level.

4. Find support

This demo cannot send alerts or arrange follow-up. In India, call 14416 or 112 for support.
